How much does a website cost?

The website format, development approach, page count and integrations affect the price. For template-based work, we also account for the template licence. We agree on scope and price after discussing the brief.

How do templates and custom development differ?

With a template, we adapt an existing foundation to your brand and content. With custom development, we plan the structure and design from scratch. The choice depends on your brief, budget and required features.

How long does development take?

We set a schedule after reviewing the brief. It accounts for website size, content preparation and design approvals. We agree on the timeline before work begins.

How do payments work?

We agree on payment terms and milestones before starting. The provider is a Belarusian professional income tax payer and issues receipts through the tax application for income received.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Domain registration and hosting are paid separately unless the proposal states otherwise. We help prepare the website for your chosen hosting. You retain ownership of your domain.

Can we add features after launch?

Yes. Changes to the agreed scope and new features are discussed separately. Support terms are listed in the proposal.

Do we receive the source code?

Yes. We hand over the code and instructions. Third-party templates, fonts and components remain subject to their licences. We agree on those terms before purchase and development.
